Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers, no fine print.

Is CancerNotes free to use?

Yes. CancerNotes is free for both iPhone (iOS) and Android, with no in-app purchases in the current build.

Do I need to create an account or sign in?

No. CancerNotes requires no account, no sign-up, and no login. Your notes, saved articles, quiz history, and custom topics are stored only on your device.

Does CancerNotes provide medical advice?

No. CancerNotes is an information and learning tool, not a medical tool. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace a care team. Always talk to your care team about your health.

Where do the news articles, tips, and quizzes come from?

Trending gathers and links to recent articles from third-party publishers (roughly the past month, with duplicates removed)—CancerNotes does not write or verify them. Quick Tips and Quizzes are AI-generated educational content created for learning only; always check with your care team.

Does CancerNotes work offline?

Yes, for content on your phone: your notes, saved articles, quiz history, and any tips or quizzes you have opened before open instantly offline. Trending news and fetching brand-new tips or quiz sets require an internet connection.

How many cancer types are included?

CancerNotes includes 118 cancer types, covering common cancers as well as rare and childhood cancers. You can also add unlimited custom keywords in Settings to follow specific treatments, trials, or topics.
