Quick Support Answers

Can you reset my account or recover deleted notes?

Because CancerNotes has no user accounts and stores your notes, saved articles, and quiz history only on your phone, we cannot access or recover data that was deleted or lost when uninstalling the app.

Why does Trending news say I'm offline?

Your notes, saved articles, quiz history, and previously opened tips and quizzes work offline, while Trending news requires an active internet connection to fetch recent articles.

Can CancerNotes answer medical questions?

No. CancerNotes is an educational and personal organisation app only. Please reach out to your doctor or care team for any medical questions.
