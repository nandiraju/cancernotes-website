Privacy Policy

Your notes, saved articles, and quiz history are stored on your device and no account is required.

On-Device Storage

Notes, saved articles, quiz history, and custom topics stay on your phone—not in a cloud profile.

No Sign-Up Ever

CancerNotes requires no account, no login, no email address, and no personal profile.

Minimal Network Use

Topic names are sent only when needed to fetch trending news, quick tips, and quiz questions.

1. Privacy by Architecture

CancerNotes is designed as an information and personal organisation app for patients, caregivers, families, and learners. We do not ask you to create an account, sign in, or share your name, email address, or medical records.

2. What Lives on Your Device

The following information is stored locally on your phone only:

  • Private Notes: Titles, descriptions, timestamps, and any questions or appointment notes you write or dictate using your phone's keyboard.
  • Saved Articles: Bookmarks you save from the Trending news tab.
  • Quiz History: Your completed quiz topics, difficulty levels, scores, and answer reviews.
  • Custom Topics & Preferences: Any custom keywords you add in Settings and your preferred quiz length.
  • Cached Tips & Quizzes: Previously opened tips and quiz sets stored locally so they open quickly offline.

Because this data is stored only on your device, CancerNotes does not sync across devices or back up your notes to a remote server.

3. What Is Sent Over the Internet

When you open Trending news, Quick Tips, or generate a new Quiz, the app sends the selected cancer type or custom topic name to our content service in order to retrieve:

  • Recent news articles from third-party publishers.
  • AI-generated educational Quick Tips cards.
  • AI-generated educational quiz questions and explanations.

Your personal notes, saved reading list, and quiz scores are never attached to these requests.

4. Third-Party Publishers & External Links

Tapping a news card in Trending opens the full article from a third-party publisher in an in-app browser. Those external websites operate under their own privacy policies and terms, which we encourage you to review.

5. Website Preferences

This website uses your browser's local storage solely to remember your light or dark theme preference (cn-theme). We do not use advertising trackers on this website.

6. Complete Control in Your Hands

Edit or Delete Anytime

You can edit or delete any note, remove any saved article, delete any past quiz result, or remove custom topics directly inside the app.

Complete Erasure

Uninstalling the CancerNotes app from your iPhone or Android device removes all locally stored notes, saved articles, quiz history, and preferences.

7. Children's Privacy & Policy Updates

CancerNotes does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because no account or personal profile is created, no personal data is collected on our servers. If we update this Privacy Policy, we will post the revised version on this page with an updated effective date.
